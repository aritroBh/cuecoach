# Detector data sources and licenses

Real Roboflow downloads must be documented here before they are used in training. `import_roboflow.py` reads the Universe project's published license before download and rejects unknown, missing, non-commercial, and no-derivatives licenses. It writes the dataset version, source, license, and attribution to this table and to a local manifest. Downloaded data stays under the root `tools/datasets/` ignore rule.

| Dataset | Version | License | Attribution | Use |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| None imported | — | — | — | Baseline uses locally rendered synthetic images only. |

The synthetic scenes are generated by `generate_dataset.py` from code and seeded randomness; no photo datasets, stock images, or game footage are bundled. Accepted reuse licenses are CC BY 2.0, CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0, CC0 1.0, and Public Domain. CC BY sources require attribution in the registry and local manifest; they are not automatically committed.
